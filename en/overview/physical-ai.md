# What is Physical AI?

Physical AI is **AI that creates actions in the real (physical) world**. It receives sensor inputs such as cameras, LiDAR, tactile feedback, and force/torque signals. It controls actuators such as robotic arms, mobile robots, and drones.

While traditional AI exists only in digital environments, Physical AI **interacts with the actual physical environment through sensors and actuators**. This enables real-time decision-making and autonomous learning.

Physical AI is not merely a technology, but represents a structural shift across the entire economy. It is the convergence of AI, edge computing, robotics, and spatial intelligence to create **autonomous systems that operate with minimal human intervention**.
