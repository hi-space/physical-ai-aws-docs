# Physical AI on AWS

### About this guide

Physical AI has recently gained significant attention, but as it encompasses multiple technologies, understanding the entire technology stack at a glance is not straightforward. Physical AI is not simply a single AI model, but rather **an integrated system where AI interacts with and operates in the physical world**.

This guide is designed to help you systematically understand the core technological elements of Physical AI and learn how to implement it practically on AWS cloud infrastructure. It covers the entire pipeline from data collection through training, simulation, Sim2Real, deployment, and agentic orchestration.

***

### Intended audience

* **Cloud/Infrastructure engineers** managing Physical AI workloads
* **AI/Platform engineers** in robotics, autonomous driving, and drone teams
* **Tech Leads / PMs** interested in Physical AI technology
